## Mandatory read set

Load stage-applicable populated/confirmed synthesis and PRFs, decisions/approvals, traceability/coverage, handbook state, immutable prior artifacts, confirmations, package members, and exact stage fingerprints.

## Semantic write targets

Only stage-owned populated handbook payloads, approved-decision projection, equivalence suite, candidate/outer payload manifests, scope certificate, package assembly, or explicitly authorized certification envelopes.

## Allowed assurance/audit side effects

Authorized `18` confirmation effects, required package/index bindings, and append-only `0G`.

## Forbidden mutations

No Validation/Gate reports, decision invention/reinterpretation, forensic/synthesis fact edits, post-candidate handbook edits without restart, secrets/private exports, or pre-step-6 Exit E pass.

## Deterministic validation

Validate HBK IDs/path/anchors, semantic hashes, upstream synchronization, deterministic chapter non-vacuity/link/diagram/CAP coverage before candidacy, candidate-bound human readability review only during confirmation, equivalence mapping, exact field order/check registries/evidence sets/snapshot equality/hash domains/member fingerprints, §15.4 stage order, and §15.5 final verification.

## Completion condition

Only the declared stage completes. Exit E passes solely after Validation/Gate executes every required final check and validates the signed step-6 outer payload. Any final verification receipt remains a non-authoritative derivative outside the certified package and protocol state; no authoritative post-step-6 artifact is emitted.
